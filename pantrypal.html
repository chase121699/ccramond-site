Overview

Scan it in when you unpack groceries, scan it out when you use it. PantryPal does the rest.

PantryPal tracks every item across your fridge, freezer, pantry and wine & spirits cellar, including the jar that's only half full. It estimates expiration dates from food-safety guidelines, reminds you a few days ahead, and builds shopping lists from whatever is running low.

Everyone at home shares the same inventory through iCloud, so the person at the store always knows what's already in the house. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account.

Features in version 1.0

Scan everything in

  • Barcode scanning for groceries from around the world, with product details filled in for you
  • Snap a photo when there's no barcode, like produce or leftovers
  • Scan a store receipt to add a whole shopping trip at once
  • Multipacks count as single items: a 12-pack is 12 cans
  • Ready-made templates for meat, produce, herbs, spices and takeout

Never lose track

  • Full and partly used amounts ("2 full, ½ open")
  • Fridge, freezer, pantry and a wine & spirits cellar
  • Sort and group by category, expiration, age or low stock
  • Search by name, brand, category or barcode
  • Health grades, plus flags for staples, favorites and treats

Use it before you lose it

  • Expiration dates estimated from food-safety guidelines, adjusted for where it's stored and when it was opened
  • Reminders a few days ahead, plus a weekly look at what's expiring
  • A "use soon" view so nothing gets forgotten at the back of the fridge

Shop smarter

  • Shopping lists built from what's low or out
  • Check items off in the store, then check your whole cart into inventory in one step
  • Recipes show what you already have and add the rest to a list
  • Scan recipes from cookbooks or recipe cards with your camera

Made for households

  • Share your inventory and lists with family or roommates through iCloud
  • Rate items and recipes 1–5 in quarter steps, and see the household average
  • Changes sync across iPhone and Apple Watch

Quick actions everywhere

  • Siri: "Use milk in PantryPal" or "Add eggs to my PantryPal list"
  • Home Screen widgets and Control Center buttons to scan in or out
  • Print QR labels for things you grab often, like coffee pods, and scan one to use it
  • Apple Watch: check off your list, use an item with a tap, and see what's expiring

Private by design

Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. Photos, receipts and recipes are read on your iPhone. No account to create, no ads, no tracking.
